# Overview

This is the first project of my first year at ISART. The goal was to develop a shoot'em up game from scratch in Flash and design a single level for it.

The main idea behind the game is a Shoot'em up where your special ability is to send back projectiles by parrying at the right time.

## Key Features

- **Parry System**: Parry at the right timing to send back enemy projectiles right back at them !
- **Enemy Variety**: 3 Different enemy types with their own movement and shooting patterns !
- **Boss Fight**: A challenging multi-phase boss encounter at the end of the level.

## My Roles

- **Game Design**: Concept, mechanics, and level layout.
- **Programming**: Implemented all systems (movement, parry, enemy AI) in ActionScript 3.
- **Art**: Created all geometric assets and UI.
