# Overview

This is our jam submission to the ISART Digital Game Week 2023. It's a real-time strategy / tycoon game where you manage and protect a hive, collect pollen and make honey!

We won one of the awards for **"Most Polished"**.

The jam theme was **"One for All, All for One"**.

## Key Features

- **Hive Management**: Manage your bee colony and ensure the hive's survival.
- **Resource Collection**: Send bees to collect pollen from flowers and convert it into honey.
- **Strategic Gameplay**: Balance resource management with hive protection in real-time.
- **Polished Experience**: Winner of the "Most Polished" award at the ISART Digital Game Week 2023.
- **Theme Integration**: Built around the "One for All, All for One" concept with colony cooperation.

## My Tasks

- **Gameplay Programming**: Implemented core gameplay mechanics and systems.
- **AI Programming**: Developed simple AI behaviors for bee units and hive management.
- **System Integration**: Connected various game systems for cohesive gameplay experience.
