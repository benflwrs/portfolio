# Overview

This is the second project of my first year at ISART. We had to develop a sokoban-inspired game in teams of 5 in C#.

You play as a wizard cleaning his castle and tidying up his magic crates. In order to do this, he can use magic to switch position with his walls but be careful as you cannot switch with crates and the walls block them.

## Key Features

- **Puzzle Mechanics**: Unique mechanic where you can switch positions with walls.
- **Level Design**: 2 Custom designed levels.
- **Level Editor**: Custom string parser for level creation.

## My Tasks

- **Visual Assets**: Designed all in-game visual assets and animations (Juiciness).
- **Programming**: Worked on the datamodel, Undo-Redo feature, and level editor tools.
- **Level Design**: Designed the first and last levels.
