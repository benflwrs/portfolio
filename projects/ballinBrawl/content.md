# Overview

The last project of our 2nd year at ISART.

It's a turn based pool RPG Free To Play on mobile. Build and upgrade your team to battle the opposing team in more than 50 levels!

Slingshot your way to victory!

This unique game combines turn-based strategy with pool mechanics, creating an engaging mobile gaming experience. Players build and upgrade their teams to battle through progressively challenging levels.
