# Overview

This is the first project of my second year at ISART.

We had to recreate the game [RUSH](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hdEMOL2dFbg) in Unity 3D.

In this game you have to place tiles to bring the cubes to their own targets.
Learn the cubes behaviors and watch out for their timing so they don't collide or fall off.

I designed every asset and programmed everything.
I also created some shaders with Shader Graph.
Music and sound wasn't made by me although I heavily modified some sounds to create new ones and edited songs so they loop properly in-game.

## Custom Tweens using Tick system
Since the game operates on a strict timing thanks to the **Tick system**, I had to make a custom tween system that fully synchronizes with it.
It handles fast forward, slow-mo and rewind.
